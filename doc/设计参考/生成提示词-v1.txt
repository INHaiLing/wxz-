Use case: ui-mockup
Create ONE polished, high-fidelity UI concept board for an existing Chinese WeChat mini-program for 专升本语文 learning. This is an original visual redesign, not a screenshot recolor. One wide landscape raster image, approximately 2400×1440, with FOUR equally sized tall flat app screens in a neat horizontal row. Large enough Chinese text to inspect. Show full screens, no perspective, no photographic phone hardware, no device notches, no hand, no 3D scene. Soft neutral board background. Each screen is proportioned like 390×844 and has a WeChat status bar, compact native capsule at the top-right, appropriate safe area, and a modest home indicator. Outside the screens, a small editorial title “墨蓝 · 纸白” and subtitle “专升本语文｜学习界面视觉提案”, four small captions “01 学习首页”“02 文言目录”“03 翻译背诵”“04 收藏复习”. A small palette strip at the bottom, hex codes as given, and label “概念方案 · 演示数据”. The four interfaces have an identical coherent design system.

Palette: primary ink navy #2F4B6E; warm paper background #F7F5F0; clean white cards #FFFFFF; main text #243244; supporting text #5D6976; pale answer panel #EDF1F6; fine divider #E4E7EB; restrained warm bronze-gold #9B6A2E ONLY for favorite stars; muted jade #2F6B55 ONLY for mastered state, accompanied by check mark and text. NO bright green theme, lime, neon, saturated gradients, large dark reading surfaces, faux parchment texture, mountains, bamboo, calligraphy illustrations, gaming elements, or decorative mascots. Modern, literate, understated Chinese educational app. Crisp typography, excellent contrast. Chinese sans-serif for body, subtle editorial weight for titles. Comfortable body text equivalent to 17–18 logical px, line-height about 1.7. Generous but practical whitespace, screen side margins 20 logical px, 16–20 px card padding, soft 14 px corners, almost no shadow. All visible wording must be clean correct simplified Chinese, not pseudo text. Small stars have generous invisible tap regions; mastering is a distinct labeled control, not another star.
